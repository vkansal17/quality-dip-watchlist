How to read this: the 52-week bar shows where the price sits between its low and high; "off high" is the size of the dip. Volatility is annualized from daily closes. P/E uses SEC-reported diluted EPS (TTM) where computable — blank where earnings are negative or filings don't map cleanly. RSI is the 14-day Wilder relative strength index. VPT 20d is the 20-day change in the Volume-Price Trend line, normalized by the period's total VPT movement — positive means volume has been flowing in on up days (accumulation). Rel Str 20d is the stock's 20-day return minus the S&P 500's — short-term relative strength versus the market. ROIC is return on invested capital from SEC filings: operating income after tax ÷ (debt + equity − cash); blank where filings don't provide the pieces (financials' capital structure makes classic ROIC less meaningful for banks/insurers). Analyst consensus/targets are aggregator figures (TipRanks / MarketBeat / StockAnalysis), curated — the crowd's opinion, not a forecast; ⚠ marks a name whose consensus or target contradicts the dip thesis. Fortune 500 ranks are Fortune's official 2026 list (by revenue); "—" = not on the list (foreign-HQ, below the revenue cutoff, or unclassified). Researched exclusions: NKE and TSLA are deliberately kept off — their dips trace to deteriorating or polarized business stories, not situations. Research and education only — not investment advice.
