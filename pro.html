How to read this: the composite grade is a percentile blend, not a recommendation. Revisions uses reported EPS-surprise history (mean surprise and beat rate over the last four quarters) — the free proxy for analyst revision momentum; clean revision-breadth history is paid data, and it is the first upgrade this lens would buy. Safety blends the Piotroski F-score (9 filing signals: profitability, leverage, liquidity, dilution, margins, turnover), net debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, current ratio and accrual quality. Value blends FCF yield, earnings yield, EV/EBITDA and price/sales. Balance-sheet factors use industrial-company definitions — read them cautiously for banks, insurers and REITs, whose filings structure debt differently; the F-score veto therefore applies only to companies that report a cost-of-sales line. Fundamentals are the latest reported fiscal year from SEC EDGAR. Research and education only — not investment advice.
