The Iran-war oil shock (Brent +85% YTD, diesel +67% y/y) and great-power supply-chain rivalry are repricing energy security, defense and critical minerals. Origin thesis: companies the shock pays — refiners, tanker owners, uranium and rare-earth suppliers, defense primes — rather than companies it taxes.

Watch: Ceasefire or escalation headlines, OPEC spare capacity, defense appropriations into the midterms, China export controls on rare earths and gallium.

Watch: Oct 28 FOMC, Dec hike pricing, core PCE trend, whether the 10-year holds above or breaks below 5%.
