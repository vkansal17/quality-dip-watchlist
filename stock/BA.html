🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #34

Boeing's turnaround is real but unfinished: 737 output is transitioning to 47/month, the MAX 7 was certified in August and the MAX 10 on Oct 20, 2026, and the backlog is a record ~$715B — yet the company still posts core losses (Q2 EPS -$0.76 missed), carries ~$45.9B of debt with negative equity, and the 777X remains uncertified with first delivery pushed to 2027. At ~$188 (Oct 23), down ~26% from the $254.35 high, the setup is balanced rather than compelling ahead of Q3 results on Oct 27.

Key risks:

  • 777X certification slips beyond early 2027, extending the widebody revenue gap after the earlier $4.9B charge
  • FAA halts or slows the 737 rate ramp on quality findings
  • High leverage leaves little cushion if deliveries or services margins disappoint

What would change this verdict: Two consecutive quarters of positive FCF with core EPS approaching breakeven and a firm 777X certification date would upgrade quality/risk; a withdrawn FCF guide would downgrade.
