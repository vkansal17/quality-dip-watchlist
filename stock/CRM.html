🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #103 (C+)

Salesforce is monetizing agents faster than the bears assumed: fiscal Q2 2027 revenue rose 10.8% to $11.35B, Agentforce ARR topped $1.5B (+240% YoY, ~$3.9B combined with Data 360), cRPO grew 14%, and management raised FY27 revenue guidance to $46.1–46.4B. The offsets are balance-sheet and model risk: the headline FY27 EPS guide ($16.67–16.71) is flattered by investment gains, and the company funded a $25B accelerated buyback with $25B of new debt while the seat-based model disruption question stays open. At $248.57 the de-rated multiple (~5x sales) prices much of this, but the rubric lands HOLD.
