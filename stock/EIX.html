🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #207 · Pro pool #76 (B)

The regulated core is healthy — Q2 2026 core EPS of $1.54 beat the $1.18 consensus, FY26 core EPS guidance of $5.90–6.20 was reaffirmed, and the long-term target is 5–7% core EPS growth on ~7% rate-base growth — and at $57.35 the stock trades ~9x forward core earnings (vs a 13x five-year average) with a ~6.1% dividend yield. What the rubric cannot clear is the still-unquantified Eaton Fire liability: SCE's voluntary program has offered ~$1.1B with ~80% acceptance, but the company has not published a total loss estimate, California's liability-reform bill died Aug 31, and a short-seller scenario above $20B (vs a ~$4.3B shareholder cap under standard imprudence findings) keeps the tail open.
