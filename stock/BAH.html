🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #66 · Pro pool #121 (C+)

Booz Allen is becoming more profitable on a shrinking top line: fiscal Q2 FY27 (reported Oct 23) revenue fell 3.9% to $2.78B as civil revenue dropped 15% on smaller recompetes and delayed starts, yet adjusted EPS rose 18% to $1.76, EBITDA margin reached 11.3%, and management raised FY27 guidance to $6.50–6.70 EPS and $850–925M FCF. At $78.32 (~12x that guide, 28% below the $109.10 high) the price is undemanding and national security demand is strong (funded backlog +10%, pipeline +20%), but a business guiding its civil segment to a low-double-digit full-year decline — with headcount already cut from 35,800 to ~31,500 — has not yet shown revenue stabilization.
