🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Pro pool #184 (D+)

Nasdaq just printed the theme's cleanest quarter: Q3 2026 net revenue rose 13–14% to $1.48B, Solutions revenue grew 17–18% to $1.17B, ARR hit a record $3.37B, Index revenue jumped 39% on $141B of trailing-twelve-month inflows, and non-GAAP EPS rose 18% to $1.04. The problem is entry price — at $102.56 the stock sits 0.7% below its high at roughly 28x trailing earnings, so the rubric pays for quality it can already see and lands HOLD on valuation.
