🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Cboe is compounding through the volatility regime rather than despite it: Q2 2026 set records (net revenue $731.6M, +25% y/y; adjusted EPS $3.56, +45%), management raised FY26 organic revenue growth guidance to the mid-to-high teens, and Q3 volumes stayed near records (SPX ADV 4.9M, record SPX 0DTE ADV of 3.2M). At $340.42 the multiple is full (~26.5x trailing, consensus target below the price as crowd context), which caps valuation at mixed, but with 0.7x leverage, $2.3B of adjusted cash and the Oct 30 print as a near-term catalyst, four legs pass.

Key risks:

  • Volumes are partly volatility-linked — a calm, falling-volatility tape would slow ADV growth from near-record levels and expose the full multiple
  • Regulatory/political scrutiny of 0DTE options and event/prediction contracts is a growing overhang on its fastest-growing products
  • The Cboe Australia divestiture (closed Jul 31) trims a diversifying revenue stream

What would change this verdict: An Oct 30 miss or guidance implying volume normalization to low-single-digit growth would cut quality/catalyst to HOLD; sustained double-digit organic growth at current capture rates would firm up the BUY.
