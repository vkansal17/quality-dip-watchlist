🔬 Research-agent verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #49 (B+)

The user engine is intact — Q2 2026 DAUs rose 23% to 58.7M with retention at an all-time high and paid subscribers up 17% to 12.7M — and the stock has de-rated ~56% from its $322 high to $143.04. But the growth model is deliberately decelerating: bookings grew only 8%, bookings per DAU fell ~12%, FY26 bookings growth is guided to just 10–12%, and management is prioritizing user growth over monetization precisely as free LLM tutors raise the long-run pricing question, so the evidence supports watching the Nov 4 print rather than buying the dip outright.

Key risks:

  • Free LLM-based tutors (ChatGPT and peers) compress willingness to pay for language learning, capping conversion and pricing power
  • Bookings growth decelerates into single digits as the reactivated-user cohort monetizes poorly, forcing another guidance reset

What would change this verdict: A Q3 print showing bookings growth re-accelerating with stable per-user monetization would flip quality and valuation to pass and justify BUY; another bookings miss would argue SELL.
