🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #31 (B+)

ResMed's core sleep business is intact — Q4 FY26 revenue rose ~9% to $1.46B with non-GAAP EPS of $2.95 beating consensus, its fourth straight beat — and company data show GLP-1 patients are ~11pp more likely to start PAP therapy with higher resupply rates, undercutting the cannibalization fear. But FY27 revenue guidance of $5.75–5.85B disappointed in August (including a $75M Astral safety-suspension headwind), and at $252.86 the stock sits essentially at the ~$252 consensus target, leaving only 3 legs passing.

Key risks:

  • Astral ventilator safety suspension costs $75M of FY27 revenue and the platform is sunset after FY27
  • Philips' return to the US CPAP market could pressure device/mask share and margins
  • GLP-1 narrative remains contested — a low-end analyst target of $175 prices in real demand erosion

What would change this verdict: A Q1 FY27 beat with FY27 guidance raised or device growth accelerating would lift quality/valuation toward BUY; a guidance cut or Philips share gains would argue SELL.
