HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #31 (B+)

ResMed's core sleep business is intact — Q4 FY26 revenue rose ~9% to $1.46B with non-GAAP EPS of $2.95 beating consensus, its fourth straight beat — and company data show GLP-1 patients are ~11pp more likely to start PAP therapy with higher resupply rates, undercutting the cannibalization fear.
