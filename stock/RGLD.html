🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Dip pool #157 · Pro pool #447 (D-)

Royal Gold is the hedge trade without the mine problem: Q2 2026 revenue rose 115% YoY to $451M and operating cash flow hit a record $335M (+119%) after the Sandstorm/Horizon acquisitions, with 2026 gold sales guided 290–320k oz (~+32%) and a five-year GEO outlook above that. The royalty model has no AISC exposure — gold-price inflation flows to revenue, not costs — the portfolio is diversified (no asset >13% of revenue), and management expects the revolver fully repaid in Q4, leaving the geopolitical-hedge exposure on a near-debt-free balance sheet.
