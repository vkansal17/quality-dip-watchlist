🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Nutanix closed FY26 (Jul) with a clean beat — Q4 revenue $757.1M vs $725–745M guidance, non-GAAP EPS $0.60 vs ~$0.49 consensus, ARR +16% to $2.55B and FY26 FCF of $841M at a ~29% margin — and guided FY27 to $3.18–3.23B revenue (~+12%) with 24–25% non-GAAP operating margin. The catch is price: after a ~74% six-month run the stock sits near its 52-week high and the $73.07 consensus target is barely above the $70.58 close, so valuation is the one leg that does not pass.
