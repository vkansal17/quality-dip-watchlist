🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Pro pool #320 (D)

Alcoa's operations are genuinely improving — Q3 2026 adjusted EPS of $1.82 beat the $1.51 consensus, revenue rose ~24% YoY to $3.70B, FCF was $472M, and full-year aluminum guidance was reaffirmed — and at $37.91 (−54.8% from the $84.38 high, ~6.7x trailing earnings) the price is deeply discounted. The theme fit, however, only half-survives contact with the filings: Alcoa is a net payer of Section 232 tariffs on its large Canadian smelter base (only ~345kt of its 2,645kt capacity is in the US), its Alumina segment is loss-making with a cut production outlook, and EBITDA already fell sequentially on weaker metal prices — cheap, but not yet BUY-justified.

Key risks:

  • Aluminum/alumina prices weaken further — Q3 already showed EBITDA falling on price despite record production at several sites
  • AliGroup integration and post-close leverage land just as the metal cycle softens, or Canadian tariff costs rise again instead of easing as guided

What would change this verdict: Two quarters of sequential EBITDA recovery with the alumina outlook restored would lift quality toward BUY; a metal-price break that pushes FCF negative would argue SELL.
