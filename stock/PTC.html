HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #50 (B+)

PTC is now a merger-arbitrage situation, not a fundamental compounder: on Oct 5, 2026 Schneider Electric agreed to acquire it for $205.00/share in cash (~$22.6B equity value, close expected Q3 2027), and at $193.80 the remaining gross spread is only ~5.8% over up to ~11 months, with analysts (Rosenblatt, Baird) moving to Hold. The underlying business is healthy — constant-currency ARR ex-divested Kepware/ThingWorx grew ~9% and FY26 ARR guidance midpoint was raised — but upside is contractually capped and deal-break downside to the ~$144 pre-announcement price is asymmetric.
