🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · carried from Validated Top 50 research

EQT is executing like the low-cost leader it claims to be: it generated $330M of Q2 free cash flow at just $2.89 gas, raised FY26 production guidance by ~90 Bcfe while trimming capex, and cut net debt from $7.7B at YE2025 to $5.7B by Q1 — nearing its ~$5B target and unlocking buybacks. Near-term EPS is falling with gas prices (Q3 expected ~$0.38 vs $0.52 a year ago, estimates revised down), which keeps quality at mixed, but at $55.63 (~12.3x earnings, ~18% off the high) the balance sheet, contracted power/data-center demand deals and LNG optionality carry the case.
