🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #48

BridgeBio's commercial engine is real — Attruby U.S. sales tripled to $222.4M in Q2 2026 and total revenue rose 120% YoY to $243.7M — and the July $1B preferred financing lifted pro-forma cash to ~$1.7B to fund three pending launches. But roughly 90% of revenue rides on a single drug, the company is still loss-making (Q2 EPS −$0.78, a miss vs −$0.64 consensus), and the next leg depends on binary FDA decisions (BBP-418 PDUFA Nov 27, 2026; infigratinib Feb 4, 2027) at a premium ~12x EV/sales — a strong story that stays HOLD until the pipeline de-risks.
