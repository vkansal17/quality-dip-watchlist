Manhattan Associates is compounding with unusual visibility: Q2 2026 cloud revenue grew 26%, RPO rose 23% to $2.47B on a third consecutive quarter of record bookings, and management raised FY26 guidance (revenue $1.160–1.166B, adjusted EPS $5.44–5.50, RPO toward the high end of $2.62–2.68B) while its AI agents show 100% pilot-to-subscription conversion so far. The balance sheet is debt-free with a ~30% FCF margin and $275M of YTD buybacks. The honest caveat is price — at $238.19, essentially at its high, the stock trades ~44x the adjusted EPS guide, so valuation is mixed rather than a pass.
