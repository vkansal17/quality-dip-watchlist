HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Dip pool #86 · Pro pool #550 (F)

The restructuring is measurably working: Q3 2026 revenue hit a decade-high $23.85B (+8%), EPS of $2.85 beat the $2.65 consensus, ROTCE reached 11.5%, Services and Wealth each grew 15%, expenses fell, and Citi returned $4.1B in the quarter under a new $30B buyback with CET1 at 12.8%.
