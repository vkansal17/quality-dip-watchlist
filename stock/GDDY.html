🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #86 (B)

GoDaddy is compounding steadily: Q2 2026 revenue rose 6.6% to $1.30B, GAAP EPS of $1.83 beat consensus, normalized EBITDA margin expanded to 33.4%, and management narrowed FY26 revenue guidance to $5.215–5.255B while reaffirming ~$1.8B of free cash flow. At $112.79 (verified Oct 23 close) the stock trades at ~15.8x trailing earnings with the share count down 11% year over year (142M to 127M) on buybacks, and the Airo AI platform's bookings run rate quintupled to $50M in one quarter. The SMB customer base is a watch item, but nothing in the numbers shows deterioration.
