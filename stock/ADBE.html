🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Adobe's reported numbers contradict the disruption narrative so far: Q3 FY26 revenue hit a record $6.76B (+13% YoY), non-GAAP EPS rose 15% to $6.13, and management raised FY26 guidance (revenue ~$26.6B, non-GAAP EPS $24.45–24.50), with AI-first ARR up >150% to >$650M. At $263.82 the stock is ~27% below its 52-week high and trades at roughly 11x FY26 guided non-GAAP EPS — a historically low multiple for a ~45% operating-margin franchise. The honest caveats are decelerating net new ARR (down sharply YoY on third-party estimates) and RPO growth slowing to 8%, plus a CEO transition on Dec 1.
