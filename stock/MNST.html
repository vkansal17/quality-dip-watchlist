HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #34 (B+)

Monster Beverage is compounding at record levels — Q2 2026 net sales rose 20.2% to a record $2.54B with international up 34.6% to 46% of sales and four straight EPS beats — and the energy-drink category keeps growing double digits despite the weak consumer backdrop. The offset is price: at $46.19 (Oct 23 close, post the August 2-for-1 split) the stock trades near ~43x trailing earnings with post-split analyst targets only ~10% overhead, so quality is not matched by margin of safety.
