🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #36

The underlying business is healthy — fiscal 2026 net bookings were a record $6.72B (+19%) with recurrent consumer spending +17%, and FY27 bookings guidance of $8.0–8.2B (+~20%) was reiterated — but the stock is essentially a binary position on the Nov 19, 2026 GTA VI launch, a date that has already slipped twice. At $203.69 (≈23% below the $265.94 high, ~35x the FY27 adjusted EPS guide of $5.75–6.00) much of a successful launch is arguably priced, and near-term guidance keeps trailing the Street (Q2 bookings guide $1.62–1.67B vs ~$1.79B consensus). Too much rides on one title and one date to clear the BUY bar before launch.
