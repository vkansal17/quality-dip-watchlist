🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #25

Goldman's Q3 2026 was outstanding — EPS $17.01 vs ~$12.86 consensus (+39% YoY), revenue $18.68B (+23%, second-highest ever), record advisory fees of $1.65B, equities revenue +72%, and 18.7% ROE — and the stock is ~24% below its high at ~12x trailing earnings. Holding it below BUY: earnings are cyclically elevated (a late-quarter deal slowdown already pushed IPOs back), provisions rose on wholesale-loan impairments, and the financials sector (map #7 Mixed/High) keeps derating despite the beats.
