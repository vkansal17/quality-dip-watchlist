HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Pro pool #183 (D+)

The theme is paying Marathon exactly as advertised — refiners led Q3 2026 energy returns (MPC +55.1% in the quarter) as Russian/Middle East capacity disruptions widened crack spreads, and Q2 delivered $8.5B of adjusted EBITDA at a 112% capture rate and 94% utilization. The problem is entry price: at $457.22 the stock is up ~181% YTD and only 5.7% off its high, trading at ~7x peak 2026 consensus EPS but over 40x fiscal 2025 EPS — the market is already capitalizing the enhanced mid-cycle management promised, so the rubric waits.

Key risks:
