🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Employment & Labor · Dip pool #90 · Pro pool #156 (C)

Rockwell is executing the automation thesis cleanly: fiscal Q3 2026 sales rose 8% (organic +10%) to $2.31B, adjusted EPS of $3.49 beat the $3.38 consensus, and management raised FY26 guidance to 7.5–9.5% sales growth and $13.00–13.30 adjusted EPS, with demand broadening into automotive, life sciences and data centers. What blocks a BUY is price: at $426.11 the stock is ~32x the raised FY26 EPS guide (~40x trailing) despite the −13.9% pullback, and the average analyst target of ~$468 (crowd context) leaves under 10% headroom if execution slips.
