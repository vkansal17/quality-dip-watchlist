Check Point is the cheap, cash-generative end of cybersecurity: Q2 2026 EPS beat at $2.55 with a 37.9% net margin and ~$2.6B of remaining performance obligations, even though revenue growth was only +1.3% and slightly missed consensus. After the Oct 22–23 vulnerability-driven sector rally the stock closed Oct 23 at $159.03 — still ~24% below its $210.66 high at only ~15–16x trailing earnings — with Q3 results on Nov 2 as the immediate catalyst.

Key risks:

What would change this verdict: A Nov 2 print showing revenue growth reaccelerating toward mid-single digits would strengthen the BUY; a guidance cut or another revenue miss would move it to HOLD.
