🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #54

Revolution Medicines is no longer purely binary: RASolute 302 doubled median survival in previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer (13.2 vs 6.7 months, HR 0.40) and Rasonque won FDA approval on Aug 26, 2026, with ~$3.9B of cash funding the launch and the zoldonrasib/RASolve pipeline. But at $186.99 the valuation already capitalizes a successful launch plus label expansion, the ~$39,800/month list price is drawing affordability scrutiny, and the company is burning cash at a $1.7–1.8B annual opex guide as a first-time commercial organization — execution, not data, is now the risk.
