🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #317 · Pro pool #85 (B)

First Solar's current economics are superb — Q2 2026 gross margin ~57%, net income $423M, $1.7B net cash and a 45.1 GW / $13.6B contracted backlog — but the earnings base is policy-built (~$2.1–2.2B of Section 45X credits inside ~49.5% guided gross margin) and bookings quality is deteriorating: the backlog shrank ~5 GW in H1 on only 2.8 GW of new bookings, and 2026 guidance was already cut in February on federal permitting delays. At $171.99, −46% off the $320.95 high and ~11x trailing earnings, price compensates for much of this, but it does not clear BUY into the Oct 29 print.

Key risks:

  • Section 232 or FEOC/domestic-content rules land with carve-outs that dilute the expected pricing benefit (Jefferies' downgrade thesis)
  • Bookings stay below shipments, shrinking the backlog and 2027–28 revenue visibility; further permitting delays hit utility customers

What would change this verdict: Backlog rebuilding with book-to-bill above 1.0 and 45X/tariff policy visibility locked in would lift quality and risk and support BUY.
