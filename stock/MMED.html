🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #32

MMED is MiniMed Group, Medtronic's diabetes business (insulin pumps, CGM, smart pens), IPO'd in March 2026 at $20 and fully separated on Oct 15, 2026 via an oversubscribed exchange offer. Growth is genuinely accelerating — FY26 revenue was a record $3.102B (+8% organic), Q1 FY27 sales rose ~16% organically with US revenue +13.1% and CGM attachment at ~69% — but the company is GAAP loss-making (TTM EPS −$1.30), standalone execution is unproven, and at $18.34 (~1.7x sales) the evidence supports only one clean pass.
