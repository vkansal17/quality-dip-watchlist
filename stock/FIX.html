🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Pro pool #191 (D+)

First, the dip check: it is real, not a split artifact — FIX's last split was a 2:1 in December 2010, and the $2,073.99 52-week high to $1,160.86 close (−44%, −30% in one month) is a genuine repricing that accelerated after Q3. The fundamentals are the strongest in this batch: Q3 2026 revenue +54% to ~$3.8B, EPS +69% to $13.91 (a beat), and backlog at a record $17.8B, +90% y/y, with data centers at 59% of revenue. What caps it is that this demand is precisely the map's #1 Negative/Severe epicenter — the sell-off came on AI-capex-slowdown fears and a founder-CFO retirement despite the beat — and at ~31x trailing earnings the stock still carries a premium multiple into that debate.
