🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Halozyme's royalty engine is inflecting: Q2 2026 revenue rose ~48% YoY to $481M with EPS of $2.28 (vs $1.79 consensus), and management raised FY26 guidance to $1.835–1.91B of revenue with royalties of $1.22–1.245B (+41–43%) and non-GAAP EPS of $8.65–9.00 — putting the stock, at its $125.63 high after an +87% YTD run, on only ~14x forward non-GAAP earnings. The patent debate is more nuanced than the headline cliff: the original rHuPH20 patent expires in the late 2020s (September 2027 per AInvest; IBD references 2029), but co-formulation/MDASE patents run far longer (the European patent just enforced against Merck expires December 2032), a Dutch court in October 2026 enjoined Merck's Keytruda SC across eight European markets, and no analyst models Keytruda royalties — making the US litigation (where a PTAB ruling did invalidate claims of one patent) upside optionality rather than a thesis dependency. The cliff is real enough to keep risk at mixed, not fail: contracts, later-expiring patents and the Hypercon acquisitions (Elektrofi, Surf Bio) bridge it.

Key risks:

  • US courts/PTAB ultimately invalidate the MDASE portfolio, killing the Keytruda royalty option and denting confidence in the wider patent estate
  • Post-2027 partner renegotiations or workarounds (e.g., Alteogen-based formulations) compress royalty rates faster than new products and Hypercon can offset

What would change this verdict: A US ruling voiding the core MDASE claims or a partner announcing an ENHANZE workaround would break the risk leg toward HOLD/SELL; a Merck license settlement would add unmodeled upside.
