🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #95 (C+)

IBM's Q3 2026 beat (revenue $17.1B, +5%; non-GAAP EPS $2.95 vs $2.87 consensus) showed the software engine working — software +8–9%, Red Hat +11%, Data +19% helped by Confluent, HashiCorp bookings growing in all geographies — and management raised the FCF outlook to >$1B growth while holding 4–5% revenue growth guidance. The offsets are real: consulting was flat with signings down, part of the software strength was Q2 deal catch-up (UBS estimates ~1% organic software growth ex catch-up/M&A), and at $253.56 the market is paying a full multiple for mid-single-digit growth with 2027 commentary deliberately withheld.

Key risks:

  • Consulting signings stay soft (book-to-bill ~0.94 in Q3) and flat consulting becomes a drag on the 2027 growth story management declined to outline
  • Software growth normalizes once the Q2 deal catch-up annualizes, exposing the ~1% organic growth UBS estimates

What would change this verdict: Evidence of organic software growth re-accelerating with consulting signings back above book-to-bill 1.0 would support BUY; another quarter of catch-up-driven software and shrinking signings would argue SELL.
