🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #5

Caterpillar's fundamentals are the strongest in years — record $72B backlog, raised FY26 guidance, and power-generation sales up 72% on data-center demand — but at ~30x trailing earnings the price still charges a perfection premium while tariffs take roughly $2.2B this year. Three legs pass with valuation and sector mixed and nothing failing: HOLD into the Oct 29 print, which will likely decide the next move.

Key risks:

  • Tariff costs (~$600M in Q3 alone) could push FY margins to the bottom of the guided range if expected recoveries disappoint
  • Power & Energy backlog is concentrated in data-center demand — AI-capex digestion or cancellations would hit the growth story directly (Truist flags durability concerns)
  • Beta ~1.6 with a premium multiple amplifies any earnings disappointment; insider selling including by the CEO has been noted into the print

What would change this verdict: An Oct 29 beat-and-raise with FY tariff costs held at or below ~$2.2B upgrades the valuation leg; a guidance cut flips quality and the verdict.
