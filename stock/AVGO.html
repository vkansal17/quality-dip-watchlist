🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #1

Broadcom's business is arguably the strongest in this batch — fiscal Q3 revenue +86% with AI semiconductor revenue +221% to $16.7B and FCF at 46% of revenue — but it fails the sector leg: semiconductors are map area #3 (Negative/Severe) precisely because the market is questioning how hyperscaler AI spending gets financed, and that spending is Broadcom's demand. At ~40x trailing earnings there is no margin of safety if the Dec 9 print or customer orders wobble, so research says HOLD despite the −33% pullback from the $495 high.
