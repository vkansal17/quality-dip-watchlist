🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Pro pool #301 (D)

Celestica's execution is the strongest in this batch: management raised the FY26 outlook in July to $20.5B of revenue and $11.30 of adjusted EPS, Q3 is guided to $5.25–5.55B (vs $3.19B a year ago) with communications revenue up ~60%, and the four-quarter average earnings surprise is +7.7%. The rubric still blocks it on the sector leg — Celestica's growth engine is hyperscaler AI hardware, which is precisely map #1 (Negative/Severe), and accelerating revenue is that sector's own variable, not a company-specific offset; the stock's −45% fall (including −29% in a month) is the market asking the same question. At $260.01, ~23x the raised FY26 EPS guide, the price is no longer the problem — the demand source is.
