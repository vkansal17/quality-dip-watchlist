Halliburton just beat Q3 2026 ($0.60 vs $0.58 consensus, revenue $5.7B) and pre-announced a credible 2027 framework: mid-single-digit revenue growth and operating margin up ~200bp to ~15%, explicitly not dependent on a North America frac recovery. It sits in the map's only Positive/Severe sector, trades at ~13.5–14.6x trailing earnings (~11.8x forward) with a ~2% dividend, and the soft North America frac market is the known, guided-for weakness rather than a surprise.
