🔬 Research-agent verdict

SELL deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #22 (A)

Norwegian has cut its 2026 outlook repeatedly: FY adjusted EPS guidance went from $2.38 to ~$1.50 and net yield is now guided down ~5% for the year (about −8.9% in Q3) as fuel costs (guided $782/metric ton net of hedges vs $670 planned) and soft Europe demand hit a company that entered the year behind its booking curve. The stock is optically cheap (~$15, ~10x the lowered guide, ~40% below its $25.13 high) and Q3 was pre-announced to beat the prior guide on revenue, but with quality and sector legs failing and JPMorgan downgrading to Underweight ($11 target) on Oct 23, the rubric says not justified.
