🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Pro pool #289 (D)

The platform is intact — fiscal Q3 2026 set records with revenue +16% to $109.4B, iPhone +22% to $54.3B and a 2.5B-device installed base — but the forward story just softened: September-quarter guidance of 9–11% came in below the ~12% expected, Services missed and slowed to +12%, and management flagged worsening advanced-node supply constraints and rapidly rising memory prices. At $347.73, essentially the 52-week high, the stock trades at ~39x trailing earnings for that decelerating guide, so valuation fails even though the business does not.
