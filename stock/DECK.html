HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #9 (A)

The Oct 22 print is the problem: despite a record $1.48B quarter, management cut FY27 revenue guidance to $5.70–5.725B from $5.86–5.91B, guided Hoka down to high-single-digit growth and Ugg to low-single-digit, and the EPS beat leaned on $0.28 of tariff refunds while DTC sales fell 1.8%. At ~10x guided EPS with analysts still at Buy (targets $92–95) the derating may be overdone, but a guidance cut plus decelerating hero brands fails the quality leg for now.
