🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #176 · Pro pool #89 (B)

Ollie's is growing the store base fast — 686 stores (+11.9% YoY, including converted former Big Lots locations), 75 openings planned, sales +9.1% in Q2 — but the core is softening: comparable sales fell 1.8% and management cut FY26 sales/comp guidance to flat-to-+0.5% comps, while the raised EPS guide ($4.57–4.65) leans on ~$28M of one-off tariff refunds. At $87.72 (verified Oct 23 close), ~20x trailing earnings and ~32% below the $130.40 high, the de-rating is partly justified by the comp trajectory. The balance sheet is clean ($507M cash, no meaningful debt), so this is a wait-for-comp-stabilization story, not a broken one.
