🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #1 (A+)

Incyte's near-term numbers are genuinely strong — Q2 product sales +40% and FY26 sales guidance raised to $5.13–5.26B on Opzelura's jump — but the rubric holds it back on two mixed legs: valuation already embeds successful diversification, and the 2028 Jakafi patent cliff (≈$3.2B of ~$5.2B sales, called 'existential' by one firm) sits inside the investment horizon even with credible mitigation underway. Only three legs pass cleanly, so HOLD is the honest verdict.

Key risks:

  • 2028 Jakafi loss of exclusivity erodes the earnings base faster than Opzelura/hem-onc can replace it
  • A failed or delayed Phase 3 readout (e.g. povorcitinib) undercuts the post-Jakafi growth story
  • Multiple compression into the cliff even on solid prints, as seen when the Q4 beat was sold on guidance

What would change this verdict: Evidence that ex-Jakafi revenue can credibly reach the guided $3–4B by 2030 (clean readouts plus Opzelura tracking above guide) would lift risk/valuation toward BUY; a pivotal readout failure or Jakafi guide cut would push toward SELL.
