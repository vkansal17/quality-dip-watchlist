HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #81 (B)

Medpace is executing superbly — Q2 2026 revenue +17.2% to $707.3M, EPS +37% to $4.25, backlog up ~28% to ~$3.0B at a 1.13x book-to-bill, and FY26 guidance raised to $2.805–2.885B revenue and $17.25–17.95 EPS — but at $669.20, essentially at its high, the stock trades at ~38x that raised EPS guide with no dip whatsoever. Analyst-modeled fair values cluster far below the price (crowd context), so this is elite quality at a price that already pays for years of flawless conversion.

  • Oncology now exceeds 50% of bookings as metabolic mix normalizes — mix shift could pressure margins
  • Client concentration: one large sponsor pulling back would move the numbers quickly

What would change this verdict: A pullback toward the low-$500s on unchanged guidance, or EPS growth sustained above ~25% making the multiple self-correcting, would support BUY.
