🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #35 (B+)

Insulet is still growing fast — Q2 2026 revenue +23.5% to $801.7M with adjusted EPS of $1.66 beating by ~14% — and the stock has already de-rated hard to $149.01 (Oct 23), down ~58% from its $354.88 high. But management cut FY26 revenue growth guidance to 20-22% on weak Type 2 retention, margins and FCF margin compressed, and over 98% of revenue rides on one product, so the evidence supports patience into the Nov 4 Q3 print rather than a BUY.

Key risks:

  • Type 2 retention fails to improve, forcing another guidance cut on Nov 4
  • FCF margin already down to 7% in Q2 from 27.4% a year earlier — cash conversion is deteriorating
  • New tubeless competitors from 2027 could pressure the Omnipod moat and the multiple

What would change this verdict: A Nov 4 print showing stabilized Type 2 retention with FY guidance reaffirmed would lift quality to pass and support BUY; another guide-down would argue SELL.
