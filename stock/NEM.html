🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #4 (A+)

Newmont just printed a record Q3 beat ($2.20 adj. EPS, $1.9B FCF) with cost guidance lowered, and at ~$4,000 gold it still earns roughly $2,500/oz of margin over guided AISC. But every leg is hostage to the metal: production fell 8.5% YoY, unit costs rose ~25% YoY, guidance was built on a $4,500 gold assumption, and spot has slipped under $4,000 on rising December Fed-hike odds. No leg fails outright, but none clearly passes either — HOLD.

Key risks:

  • Gold breaks sustainably below ~$4,000 as December Fed-hike odds rise, compressing earnings quickly
  • Continued cost inflation (AISC +25% YoY on a co-product basis) and a declining production profile
  • 2026 guidance was framed on a $4,500/oz gold assumption, above the current spot price

What would change this verdict: Gold holding or reclaiming ~$4,300 with 2027 guidance intact would upgrade it; a sustained sub-$4,000 gold price would downgrade it.
