HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #13 (A)

Intuit's FY26 closed strong (revenue +14% to $21.4B, Q4 EPS beat) and FY27 guidance was reaffirmed at the September investor day, but the guide embeds a deliberate reset: revenue growth decelerates to 9-10% with TurboTax at just 2-3% after DIY customer losses. At ~15x forward EPS the AI-disruption derating (stock ~50% below its $689 high) looks largely priced, yet with quality and sector legs only mixed, the rubric's 4-pass bar for BUY is not met.
