🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #37

DaVita beat Q2 (EPS $4.02 vs $3.88, revenue $3.55B +5.2%) yet fell ~17% as revenue per treatment dropped to $415.8 on the loss of pandemic-era ACA subsidies and lower phosphate-binder revenue, and its FY26 EPS guide of $14.10–15.20 was set below analyst estimates. Valuation is supportive (~15x trailing, ~12.5x the guide midpoint at ~$183.55, roughly 24–26% below the $247.49 high) and buybacks are aggressive, but reimbursement is a structural squeeze — CMS has proposed only a +1.1% ESRD update for CY2027 vs +2.2% for 2026, below cost inflation — and leverage runs ~3.3x. Solid, cheap, and capped: HOLD.
