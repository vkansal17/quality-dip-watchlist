🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #109 · Pro pool #98 (C+)

Demand is at record levels — Q3 2026 set records for revenue, yields and net income ($1.9B), FY26 adjusted EPS guidance was raised to $2.24, customer deposits hit a record $7.6B, and 2027 is roughly half-booked at record occupancy and pricing. The offset is the balance sheet and the fuel tape: debt is down below $24B and S&P restored investment grade, but leverage is still ~3x EBITDA into the map's Negative/Severe travel/fuel shock, and Q4 EPS guidance of $0.20 came in below consensus on fuel costs. Three legs pass, but the sector and risk legs keep it short of BUY.
