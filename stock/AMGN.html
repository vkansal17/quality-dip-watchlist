🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Amgen's growth rotation is working: Q2 2026 revenue rose 10% to $10.1B with adjusted EPS of $6.29, management raised FY26 guidance (revenue $38.2–39.4B, adjusted EPS $22.30–23.50), and Repatha (+37%), Evenity (+38%) and Uplizna (+90%) are outgrowing the guided Prolia/XGEVA biosimilar erosion. The one non-pass leg is valuation — at $439.26 the stock sits ~2% below its $447.03 high after a ~50% one-year run, ~19x guided adjusted EPS for mid-single-digit EPS growth — full price for quality, with the Nov 3 print and MariTide Phase 3 initiations as near-term tests.
