HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Dip pool #92 · Pro pool #258 (D+)

Qualcomm is mid-transition: fiscal Q3 2026 revenue fell 4% YoY to $9.95B and non-GAAP EPS fell 20% to $2.21 as handset revenue dropped 20% to $5.09B and Apple's modem insourcing accelerates, while automotive grew 61% and auto+IoT combined rose 28%. At $175.91 the stock is cheap for semis (~20x trailing, ~16x forward earnings) and Q4 guidance was maintained at $9.7–10.5B, but the core engine is shrinking and the offsets are not yet large enough to carry the rubric.
