🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

The earnings base is not shrinking — it is rotating: Q2 2026 total revenue grew 16.7% to $4.29B with non-GAAP EPS of $14.29, because Dupixent ($6B global in Q2, +38% at constant currency) and EYLEA HD (+52% US) are outgrowing the legacy EYLEA decline (−45% US) that management openly guides to continue at a low- to mid-teens pace per quarter. From Q3 the repaid Sanofi development balance steps collaboration revenue up further, and at $808.77 the stock trades at roughly 17x FY26 consensus EPS (~$47.93) — a reasonable price for double-digit growth with a ~50-program pipeline.

Key risks:

  • Legacy EYLEA declines faster than the guided low- to mid-teens per quarter as more biosimilars launch, outrunning EYLEA HD conversion
  • Dupixent growth moderates more than expected as launches annualize, removing the offset that keeps total revenue growing double digits

What would change this verdict: A Q3 miss showing combined Eylea-franchise decline overwhelming Dupixent growth would break the quality leg toward HOLD; continued double-digit revenue growth with the collaboration step-up delivered firms up the BUY.
