🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #21

MercadoLibre's growth engine is intact — Q2 2026 revenue +50% YoY to $10.17B, GMV +36% FX-neutral, Mercado Pago TPV above $100B and credit NPLs near historic lows — but management is deliberately spending the P&L: operating margin compressed to 6.7%, credit provisions rose ~85% YoY and net profit fell despite record revenue. At $1,810 (Oct 23 close) the stock is ~25% below its $2,428 high yet still ~48–50x earnings, so the Nov 4 Q3 print needs to show margin stabilization before the valuation case works.

Key risks:

  • A Brazil consumer-credit downturn would hit a credit book growing 75% YoY and force provisions higher
  • Shopee and Amazon competition in Brazil could extend the free-shipping/logistics investment cycle and delay margin recovery
  • FX depreciation in BRL/ARS/MXN can erase USD-reported growth quickly

What would change this verdict: Q3 evidence of operating-margin stabilization with NPLs contained would support BUY; a credit-quality break or another profit decline on record revenue would argue SELL.
