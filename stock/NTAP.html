🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #58 (B)

NetApp has genuinely re-accelerated: fiscal Q1 2027 revenue rose ~30% to $2.03B with non-GAAP EPS of $2.58 (+66%), FY27 guidance was raised to $7.975–8.225B revenue and $9.73–10.03 EPS, and the Novus AI storage architecture plus Oracle/Supermicro partnerships deepen its AI data-infrastructure role. But the stock has doubled YTD to $215.48 (~30x trailing earnings, already above Evercore's raised $210 target), so the re-rating is largely in the price: HOLD.
