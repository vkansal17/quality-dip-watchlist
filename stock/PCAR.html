🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #47 · Pro pool #412 (D-)

PACCAR is defending earnings near the cycle trough — Q2 2026 EPS of $1.43 beat consensus and net income rose ~4% YoY on pricing and a resilient parts business — but truck deliveries are still slipping (38,700 in Q2 vs 39,300 a year ago) and September Class 8 orders fell ~7–9.5% YoY as fleets wait for EPA 2027 NOx clarity. At $106.96 (~23x trough trailing EPS, ~15x FY27 consensus) with a fortress balance sheet and ~3% dividend yield, the −22.6% dip prices the cycle fairly, but the order inflection is not yet proven.
