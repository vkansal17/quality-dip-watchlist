🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Pro pool #177 (C)

The platform is compounding — Q3 2026 revenue rose 22% to $3.05B, adjusted EPS rose 25% to $3.00 and beat by ~14%, procedures grew ~15%, 546 systems were placed (315 of them da Vinci 5), and 84% of revenue is recurring. Two deliberate/self-inflicted items cap the verdict: a $450M pre-tax antitrust settlement charge (still subject to court approval) and management's plan to cut instrument revenue per procedure by ~3–5% from 2027 to buy penetration, while the stock still trades around ~37x trailing earnings at $452.29.
