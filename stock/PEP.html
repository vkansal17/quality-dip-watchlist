🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #35

PepsiCo beat Q3 2026 on the headline (EPS $2.34 vs ~$2.30 expected, revenue +5.6% to $25.27B) but cut FY26 core EPS growth guidance to 2.5-3.5% from the low end of 5-7%, with North American beverage volumes down 2% and margins narrowing under cost inflation — a guidance-down pattern under activist Elliott's microscope. At $135.77 (Oct 23 close), ~21% below the $171.48 high, ~17x trailing earnings and a ~4.4% dividend yield provide downside cushion, but the quality leg fails until North America stabilizes.
