🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #2 (A+)

Fox is executing at a record level — FY26 revenue and EBITDA hit highs, adjusted EPS rose 13%, and it has beaten EPS estimates four straight quarters — while trading at ~11x forward earnings with a ~10% FCF yield. The offsets are the DOJ-reviewed ~$22B Roku acquisition and secular pay-TV decline, which keep it short of a BUY under the rubric (3 legs pass, none fail). Earnings on Oct 29 and the record midterm ad cycle are the near-term proof points.
