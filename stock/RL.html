HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #11 (A)

Ralph Lauren is the evidenced exception to the weak-consumer map: brand elevation and reduced discounting are producing 73.7% gross margins, Q1 FY27 revenue +14% with Asia +24%, and raised full-year guidance while peers struggle.
