🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Dip pool #169 · Pro pool #209 (D+)

GM's ICE engine is doing exactly what the thesis asks: Q3 2026 revenue of $50.02B and adj. EPS of $3.70 beat consensus, North American EBIT rose 43%, full-year FCF guidance was raised to $11.5–12.5B, and the board added a $20B buyback on top of a completed $6B program, with the share count already down to 849M. What caps it is that guidance was narrowed, not raised (EBIT midpoint unchanged at $15B), commodity costs are tracking to the ~$2B high end, and the market sold the print ~6% on slowing-sales concern — at ~5.7x guided EPS the value is obvious, but only three legs pass cleanly.

Key risks:

  • US auto demand slows into a 5% rate backdrop while commodity/DRAM costs stay at the high end, squeezing the NA truck margins that fund everything
  • Tariff refunds and payment timing flattered 2026 FCF (management concedes some upside does not repeat in 2027), so the cash guide may overstate run-rate

What would change this verdict: A Q4 print that raises (not just narrows) 2027 guidance with tariff costs contained would lift sector/risk and support BUY; a demand-driven EBIT guide cut would argue SELL despite the multiple.
