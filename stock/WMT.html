🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Pro pool #264 (D+)

Walmart is the evidenced trade-down winner in a squeezed-consumer tape: fiscal Q2 revenue rose 5.9% to $187.9B, adjusted EPS rose 19% to $0.81, and management raised FY27 guidance (sales +4–5% cc, EPS $2.80–2.87) despite $2B+ of incremental fuel costs, while higher-margin engines compound — e-commerce +23%, advertising +38%, membership fees +17%. The honest offsets are a U.S. comp miss (+2.6% vs ~3.8% expected, with tariff refunds flattering the quarter) and a rich ~42x guided EPS multiple, which keep valuation at mixed rather than pass.

Key risks:

  • U.S. traffic and ticket growth keep decelerating (traffic +1.5% in Q2 vs +3% a year ago) as the consumer weakens further
  • Q2 profit was flattered by ~$2.9B of tariff refunds now being reinvested into price — reported profit growth slows mechanically in H2

What would change this verdict: A Q3 print on Nov 19 showing U.S. comps back toward 3.5%+ without refund support would firm the BUY; a guidance cut or negative U.S. comps would move it to HOLD/SELL.
