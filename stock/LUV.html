🔬 Research verdict

SELL deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #28

Southwest's commercial reset is producing record revenue — Q3 operating revenue of $8.42B (+21.2%) and unit revenue +21.6% — but fuel is consuming the gains: Q3 fuel expense rose $821M YoY to ~$3.92/gal with the carrier fully unhedged, and on Oct 21 management cut FY26 adjusted EPS guidance for the second time in two quarters, to $2.60–2.85 from $3.25–4.25 (and from above $4.00 earlier in the year). With the quality leg failing on repeated guidance cuts and the sector leg failing inside the map's Negative/Severe airlines area, the rubric returns SELL (not justified) despite adequate liquidity.

Key risks:

  • Further fuel escalation toward or beyond the $4.55/gal Q4 assumption, fully unhedged, cuts EPS roughly $0.08 per $0.10/gal
  • A third guidance cut would confirm the commercial reset cannot outrun costs
  • Labor and airport cost inflation (CASM-ex guided up) compounds the fuel headwind

What would change this verdict: Two consecutive quarters of held-or-raised guidance with RASM gains outpacing fuel would repair the quality leg toward HOLD/BUY; another guidance cut confirms the SELL.
