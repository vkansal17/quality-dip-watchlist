HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Pro pool #331 (D)

The RNAi platform is still growing fast — Q2 2026 revenue reached $1.29B with Amvuttra at $1.01B in the quarter and TTR revenue +89% YoY — but management cut FY26 guidance on July 30 (combined product revenue to $4.7–5.1B from $4.9–5.3B; TTR to $4.2–4.5B) as second-line ATTR-CM demand normalized, and the stock has halved to $224.03 from a $485.36 high. The de-rating makes valuation reasonable (~37x trailing, ~6x guided product sales) and the balance sheet holds ~$3.3B of cash, but a guidance cut fails the quality leg until the reset numbers are actually delivered against.
