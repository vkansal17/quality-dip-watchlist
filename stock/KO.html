🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Pro pool #214 (D+)

Pricing power is proven, not hypothetical: Q2 2026 organic revenue rose 6% with unit case volume up 5% alongside positive price/mix, and management raised FY26 guidance (organic ~5%, comparable EPS +9–10%, free cash flow ~$12.4B). The problem is the entry price — after a ~33–35% year-to-date run, $92.71 sits near the $95.37 high at ~27.9x trailing and ~26.6x forward earnings for mid-single-digit organic growth. The theme is right; the market has already paid for it, so the Oct 27 print has to justify a premium that leaves no margin of safety.
