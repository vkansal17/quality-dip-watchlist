🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #53 · Pro pool #536 (F)

Knight-Swift's Q3 2026 print (Oct 21) confirmed the freight cycle has turned: revenue +14.2% to $2.2B, adjusted EPS $0.83 vs $0.32 a year ago, and the adjusted operating ratio improved 450bp to 89.3% as supply-side capacity exits — not demand — drive pricing (revenue per mile ex-fuel +12.2%). But at $61.21 the stock already prices a good chunk of the recovery (~15x FY27 consensus EPS of ~$3.95, far more on trough FY26 earnings), and the 2027 bid-season rate story still has to be delivered against $6.20 diesel and insurance/driver cost pressure.
