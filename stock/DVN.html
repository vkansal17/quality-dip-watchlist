HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Pro pool #162 (C)

Devon is executing its post-merger harvest: Q2 2026 EPS of $1.57 beat the $1.41 consensus, full-year 2026 guidance was reaffirmed after the Coterra merger closed in May, Q3 volumes are guided to 1.66–1.69M Boe/d, and the ~$3.85B Eagle Ford sale to Crescent Energy (announced Oct 8) funds buybacks and debt reduction toward a sub-1.0x leverage target.
