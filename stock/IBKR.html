🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Dip pool #160 · Pro pool #403 (D-)

Interactive Brokers compounded straight through the cycle in Q3 2026: accounts rose 35% to 5.58M, customer equity grew 27% to $964.7B, DARTs rose 18% to 4.27M, revenues climbed ~16% to $1.87B adjusted with a 78% pretax margin, and the board raised the quarterly dividend 71% to $0.15. The toll-taker model and a debt-free balance sheet are exactly the theme fit — what holds it at HOLD is price and mix: at $80.28 the stock trades around 30x+ trailing earnings versus a ~23x five-year median, and net interest income is now over 60% of revenue heading into a possible rate-cut path.

Key risks:

  • Fed easing compresses NII (over 60% of revenue) faster than account growth offsets, while DARTs already fell 3% sequentially and stock volumes dropped 24% YoY in Q3
  • At a premium-to-history multiple, any quarter of slowing account adds or margin-loan contraction de-rates the stock quickly

What would change this verdict: A de-rating toward its historical ~23x multiple with account growth still above ~25% would flip valuation and support BUY; evidence that rate cuts are shrinking NII in absolute terms would argue SELL.
