🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #50 · Pro pool #562 (F)

Conagra's fundamentals are deteriorating: fiscal Q1 2027 revenue fell 1.4% to $2.60B with weak organic volumes, FY27 guidance calls for organic sales down 1–3% and adjusted EPS of only $1.40–1.50, and the dividend was cut 50% in July 2026 to $0.70/year. The offset is price: at ~$14–15 the stock trades near 10x guided EPS with a ~5% post-cut yield, and Q1 adjusted EPS of $0.41 actually beat the $0.28 consensus. With net debt around $7.4B against a ~$6.6B market cap, the quality leg fails but leverage is not yet a survivability cliff — a classic value-trap-versus-turnaround standoff that lands at HOLD.
