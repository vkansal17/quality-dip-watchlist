🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #81 · Pro pool #56 (B+)

Comcast is objectively cheap at $22.69 (~7.3x trailing earnings, ~30% below its high) and Q3 2026 (Oct 22) beat on EPS ($1.07 vs $0.98) with $4.2B of free cash flow, Peacock reaching a second straight profitable quarter and 368k wireless adds — but the core broadband business lost another 144,000 subscribers, worse than a year earlier, and adjusted EPS still fell 4% YoY. The dip is partly justified by a structurally shrinking core, so cheap alone does not clear BUY: HOLD.

Rubric legFindingEvidence
catalystpassThe restructuring program targeting billions in cost improvements, wireless conversion of free lines to paid (service revenue +14.7%), and the planned NBCUniversal/Versant separation path are identifiable drivers within two quarters.
qualityfailDomestic broadband lost 144k subscribers in Q3 2026 (vs 91k a year earlier) to fiber/fixed-wireless competition, broadband revenue fell 4.7%, and adjusted EPS declined 4% YoY despite beats — the core franchise is deteriorating.
riskmixedSurvivability is not in question given scale and FCF, but leverage is material and management announced a restructuring charge exceeding $1B; theme parks EBITDA fell 17.1% in Q3.
sectorpassTelecom/media is not among the map's negative areas; connectivity is defensive and non-discretionary even though the name sits adjacent to pressured consumer/media categories.
valuationpass$22.69 is ~7.3x trailing earnings near the 52-week low ($20.48) and ~31% below the $32.86 high — deep pessimism is priced in relative to $4.2B quarterly FCF.

Key risks:

  • Broadband subscriber losses accelerate beyond the Q3 pace as fiber overbuilds and fixed wireless undercut on price
  • Restructuring charges grow or savings fail to offset connectivity revenue decline
  • Theme parks and media cyclicality add earnings volatility on top of the broadband decline

What would change this verdict: Two quarters of narrowing broadband losses with wireless revenue growth offsetting connectivity declines would repair the quality leg toward BUY; losses re-accelerating past ~170k per quarter would argue SELL.
