🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #73 (B)

Ulta is executing well — Q2 FY26 sales +8.9% to $3.04B, EPS $6.55 beat, and FY26 guidance was raised (EPS $28.70–29.00, comps 3.2–3.7%) with the buyback lifted to $1.8B — and beauty demand is proving resilient. But at $624.46 the stock trades ~21.5x raised FY26 guidance after a +14.5% month, second-half guidance already embeds a slowdown (comps 2–3%), and it sits in the map's Negative/High consumer bucket, so the evidence supports quality without a margin of safety.

Key risks:

  • Second-half comp guide of only 2–3% with transactions flat — growth is ticket-driven into a promotional environment
  • Space NK mix is already compressing gross margin and competition (Sephora, Amazon, TikTok Shop) raises marketing spend
  • Inventory of ~$2.4B risks markdowns if holiday demand disappoints

What would change this verdict: A Dec 3 print with transaction growth turning positive and comps at or above guide would strengthen the case; a multiple pullback toward the high-teens on unchanged guidance would make this a BUY.
