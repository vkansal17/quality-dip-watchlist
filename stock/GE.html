What caused the dip

Sell-the-news. Q3 (Oct 20) was a beat-and-raise, yet shares fell ~3.7% that day and kept sliding (fourth straight post-earnings drop): Q3 FCF fell 14% YoY to $2.0B, commercial services growth decelerated to ~17% from 32% in H1, LEAP delivery growth slowed to ~5%, GE9X revenue deferred until FAA certification, and the stock was already rich at ~38.7x guided 2026 EPS.

Business pulse

Fundamentally strong. Adjusted EPS $2.25 (+36%) vs $2.01 consensus, adjusted revenue $13.3B (+18%), orders $19.0B (+49%, 1.35 book-to-bill), operating profit +30%; FY26 guidance raised (EPS $8.00-8.10, revenue growth 19-20%, FCF $9.1-9.4B); services backlog >$170B.

What analysts say

Strong Buy consensus among 22 analysts, average target $394.14 (+38%) — most recent compilation Sep 30; no notable October downgrades found. One model-based service rates it Hold purely on valuation.

Media & social buzz

Muted vs the chip names — media widely ran 'beat, raised, fell anyway' explainers framing it as a growth-slope repricing rather than a franchise verdict, but no prominent October 'buy the dip' call, and little social-media presence.

Risks & what would break the thesis

The bear case is 2027 aftermarket deceleration (services growth already halved in H2); casting-supply bottlenecks; GE9X certification timing; margin expansion pushed to 2028; still ~35x earnings, so little margin of safety if the slope disappoints.
