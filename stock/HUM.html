🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Pro pool #450 (D-)

The policy cycle has whipsawed Humana in both directions: weak 2026 Star Ratings imposed a ~$3.5B profit hit and cut the 2026 adjusted EPS floor to at least $9.00, but the final 2027 ratings (Oct 8, 2026) put 95% of members in 4-star-plus plans, up from ~20% for 2026, restoring 2028 bonus eligibility worth an estimated ~$3.6B of revenue. At $422.07 after a ~96% six-month run, the stock is priced on that 2028 recovery (Baird sees >$35 adjusted EPS in 2028) rather than on current earnings — ~40x trailing — so the rubric waits for the Nov 6 print and the April rate finalization to confirm the bridge.
