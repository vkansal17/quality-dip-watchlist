🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #30

Blackstone's fee machine is intact — Q3 2026 fee-related earnings +6% to $1.57B, record AUM of $1.37T (+10%) on $63.3B of inflows, and DE of $1.32 beat consensus — but distributable earnings fell 13% YoY as realizations dropped 34% in a 5% 10-year world, and BCRED has now seen three straight quarters of redemption requests (~10% of shares) above its 5% cap with NAV sliding. At $117.97 (−29% from the high, ~26x trailing vs a ~39x five-year average, ~4.4% yield) valuation passes, but quality, sector and risk are all mixed.
