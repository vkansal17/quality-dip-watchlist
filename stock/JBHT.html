🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #47

Freight volumes have clearly troughed and turned: Q3 2026 revenue rose 20% to $3.68B with intermodal loads up 8% to a record and EPS of $1.82 edging past consensus. But profit conversion is poor — operating income rose only 2%, margin compressed to 6.7% from 7.9% on $50M+ of driver, fuel and claims cost headwinds — and at $226 the stock still trades at a premium multiple on trough earnings, so the 2027 bid-season repricing story needs to deliver before this clears the bar.
