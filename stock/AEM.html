🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Dip pool #239

The real-asset hedge survives the stress test the brief demanded: Q2 AISC was $1,459/oz against a $4,483 realized price, and even at gold's Oct 23 break below $4,000 the margin is roughly $2,500/oz — profitability is not a peak-price artifact. Q2 delivered record free cash flow of $1.34B, net cash of ~$3.27B against just $197M of total debt, and a record $625M of shareholder returns, while production guidance (3.3–3.5 Moz) was maintained; at $173.94 and ~15x expected FY26 EPS, the de-rating has already happened.

Key risks:

  • Gold sustains a break below ~$3,900 toward $3,500 — miner equities de-rate faster than the metal despite the AISC cushion

What would change this verdict: A Q3 miss with production guidance cut below 3.3 Moz, or gold closing sustainably under ~$3,600, would move this to HOLD; delivery at $4,000 gold firms the BUY.
