BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Dip pool #239

The real-asset hedge survives the stress test the brief demanded: Q2 AISC was $1,459/oz against a $4,483 realized price, and even at gold's Oct 23 break below $4,000 the margin is roughly $2,500/oz — profitability is not a peak-price artifact.
