🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Dip pool #136 · Pro pool #120 (C+)

The trade-down thesis is in the numbers: fiscal Q2 comps rose 10% on traffic, sales grew 13% to $6.26B, and even excluding the $0.60 one-time tariff refund, EPS of ~$2.06 beat the company's own $1.85–1.93 guide; management raised FY26 EPS guidance to $8.61–8.77 and lifted 2026 openings to 115 stores. At $229.30 (~26x the raised guide) the multiple is full, which caps valuation at mixed — but off-price is the evidenced exception inside a negative consumer map, and the balance sheet plus a $1.275B buyback keep risk clean.
