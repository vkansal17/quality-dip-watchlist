🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #3 (A+)

RenaissanceRe is a disciplined, highly profitable underwriter (72.8% Q2 combined ratio, ~23% ROE) whose FY26 estimates are being raised into its Oct 27 report. The problem is cycle timing: the stock is near its highs at ~6x peak-cycle earnings while reinsurance pricing falls by double digits into 2027, and the analyst consensus target ($337.81) sits below the $352.60 price. Quality and catalyst pass; valuation, sector and risk are mixed, with no leg failing.

Key risks:

  • A major Q3/Q4 hurricane landfall hitting the Oct 27 print or Q4
  • Soft pricing into the Jan 2027 renewals compressing forward EPS (~$41 expected for FY27 vs ~$45.66 FY26)
  • Casualty reserve or social-inflation adverse development as terms loosen

What would change this verdict: A clean Q3 print plus firm Jan-2027 renewal commentary or stepped-up buybacks would upgrade it; heavy cat losses or accelerating price declines would downgrade it.
