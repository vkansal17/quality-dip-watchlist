🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #19 (A)

Exelixis is executing well — Q2 revenue $628.7M (+10.6%), EPS $0.91 beat, Cabometyx exclusivity now defended to January 2030 after a Federal Circuit win, $1.4B cash and active buybacks — but the stock sits at $64.53, essentially at its 52-week high after a ~47% YTD run, so there is no dip discount. Single-product concentration (Cabometyx is ~90% of product revenue) with a 2030 cliff and a delayed zanzalintinib PDUFA (now Mar 3, 2027) keep risk and valuation at mixed: 3 passes, no fails.
