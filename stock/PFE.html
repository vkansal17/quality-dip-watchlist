🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #68 (B)

Pfizer's non-COVID base is genuinely improving — Q2 2026 beat ($15.0B revenue, $0.77 adj EPS), the FY26 revenue guide was raised to $60.5–62.5B, and Padcev/Nurtec/Lorbrena are growing double digits — but 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $2.80–3.00 still implies a decline year, and the Eliquis/Xeljanz/Ibrance/Xtandi loss-of-exclusivity wave runs through 2030. At $30.03 the stock sits ~1% below its 52-week high after a ~21% one-year run, so the ~10x forward multiple and ~5.7% yield are compensation for a cliff, not a dip discount.

  • COVID product revenue steps down faster than modeled while cost savings are exhausted

What would change this verdict: Evidence that non-COVID growth can hold EPS flat-to-up through the first big LOE year — or a pullback restoring a dip discount — would support BUY.
