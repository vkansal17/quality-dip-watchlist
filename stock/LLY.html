🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #45 (B+)

Lilly's fundamentals are the strongest in this batch: Q2 2026 revenue jumped 48% to $23.0B on Mounjaro/Zepbound volume, non-GAAP EPS of $8.38 crushed the $6.01 consensus, and 2026 revenue guidance was raised to $85–87B with underlying EPS guidance lifted $2.78 at the midpoint. The problem is price, not business: at $1,273.46 the stock sits only ~4% below its 52-week high at ~42.8x trailing and ~33x forward earnings, with consensus targets only ~4% overhead — no dip and no margin of safety. Tirzepatide concentration (~$14.9B of Q2's $23.0B revenue) and net-price erosion (−13% realized prices) keep risk at mixed.
