HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #43

RTX's Q3 (Oct 20) was a beat-and-raise: sales +11% to $24.97B, adj. EPS $2.26, FY26 guidance lifted to $97.0–97.5B sales and $7.65–7.75 adj. EPS with FCF of $9.0–9.2B, and backlog hit a record $340B on ~$70B of Raytheon framework conversions and 800+ GTF orders YTD.
