🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Roivant has crossed from platform story to commercial company: Priovant's brepocitinib (Lisraya) won FDA approval on Aug 27, 2026 as the first oral therapy for dermatomyositis and launched at ~$35,000/month, and on Oct 21 the two Phase 3 CLARITY trials in non-infectious uveitis hit the primary and all key secondary endpoints (median time to treatment failure >48 weeks vs ~12 on placebo), setting up a 1H 2027 sNDA. Setbacks are real but contained (IMVT-1402's CLE proof-of-concept failed in September and was discontinued; Immunovant's TED program flunked), while the balance sheet — $1.25B of cash & equivalents plus $2.59B of marketable securities at Jun 30, 2026, plus a $950M Moderna settlement payment received in July 2026, and no debt — funds the Immunovant (IMVT-1402), Pulmovant and Genevant LNP-litigation optionality. At $36.49 (~$27B market cap, ~14% below the $42.50 high after a +100% one-year run) the price already capitalizes meaningful pipeline success, which keeps valuation mixed, but execution, breadth and cash carry the other legs.
