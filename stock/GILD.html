🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #36 (B+)

Gilead's base business is compounding: management raised FY26 product-sales guidance to $30.1–30.4B after base-business sales grew ~10% YoY in Q2, the HIV franchise is durable (Biktarvy exclusivity extended to 2036 via patent settlement), and twice-yearly Yeztugo (lenacapavir) gives a visible second growth driver in the ~$20B PrEP market. Reported 2026 EPS is distorted by ~$11.2B of acquired IPR&D charges from the Arcellx/Ouro/Tubulis deals plus a separate $1.75B impairment, but underlying operations are solidly profitable with a ~2.2% dividend yield. Valuation is the one non-pass: at $156.04 the stock sits near its $160.51 high after a +27% YTD run, so the case is quality-and-catalyst rather than dip-driven.

Key risks:

  • Oncology/cell-therapy execution — anito-cel approval delay or competitive setback vs. Carvykti would dent the diversification story
  • Policy risk: Medicare drug-price negotiation (IRA) and the ~2% ACA/Medicaid pricing headwind already embedded in HIV guidance

What would change this verdict: A Q3 miss on HIV sales or a negative anito-cel outcome would move this to HOLD; continued Yeztugo acceleration with raised guidance would strengthen the BUY.
