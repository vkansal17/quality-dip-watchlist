Baker Hughes has beaten consensus in four straight quarters (average surprise ~17.4%), with Q2 EPS of $0.64 vs $0.51 expected, firm Industrial & Energy Technology profitability and record order momentum, and it sits in the map's only Positive/Severe sector.
