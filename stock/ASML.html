What caused the dip

Oct 14 Q3: record results but revenue narrowly missed the consensus figure, shares -3% overnight and ~14% below the June high; investors also focused on the rising cost of ASML's capacity-expansion plans, plus the broader AI-unwind/rate backdrop (-7.8% that week).

🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #7

ASML the business is unambiguous — EUV monopoly, record Q3 sales of EUR11.7B at a 57.5% gross margin, full-year guidance raised three times in 2026, and demand outrunning planned capacity into 2028. ASML the stock still carries a ~55x trailing multiple and an open regulatory cliff over the ~20% of sales from China (the proposed MATCH Act), leaving only two legs as clean passes: HOLD — the research validates the company, not yet the price.

Key risks:

  • MATCH Act passage would directly cut China DUV revenue and likely compress the multiple further
  • Customer concentration is extreme — a capex pause at TSMC, Samsung or SK Hynix hits orders immediately
  • Q3 net income included a ~EUR500M investment revaluation gain; underlying growth is strong but slightly below the headline

What would change this verdict: MATCH Act passage (or its clear defeat) resolves the risk leg either way; a 2027 guidance raise with the January Q4 print would strengthen the valuation case toward BUY.
