🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #20

Abbott's Q3 2026 (Oct 21) was solid but not spectacular: revenue +14.5% reported (+7.0% comparable) to $13.01B and adjusted EPS of $1.42 in line, with the FY26 EPS guide midpoint held at $5.50–$5.55, but management cut FY comparable sales growth to ~6% from 6.5–7.5% and trimmed the Q4/2027 outlook on diabetes-care pricing and electrophysiology softness. At ~$103.70 (≈19x forward EPS, ~22% below the 52-week high) the defensive healthcare compounder looks reasonably priced, with Q4 device launches and January's first 2027 guidance as near-term catalysts and the Nutrition spin-off (by end-2027) a longer-dated structural one.
