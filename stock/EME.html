🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #289 · Pro pool #118 (C+)

EMCOR's operations are the strongest of the data-center contractors: Q2 2026 revenue rose 19.8% (19.6% organic) to a record $5.15B, EPS jumped 34.8% to $9.06, RPO hit a record $17.14B (+44%, 95% organic), and FY26 guidance was raised substantially to $20–20.5B revenue and $32–33.25 EPS. The cap is the same tension that held Sterling at HOLD, in milder form: at $647.66 the stock trades at ~20x that raised guide — a premium for a contractor — while the group is being repriced with map #1, partly offset by genuinely diversified backlog (water/wastewater, healthcare, institutional alongside data centers).
