🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

AstraZeneca beat Q2 2026 expectations with core EPS up 18% at constant currency to $2.63 on revenue of $15.38B, powered by Oncology (+16%, Tagrisso/Imfinzi/Enhertu) and Rare Disease, and reaffirmed FY26 guidance for mid-to-high single-digit revenue growth and low double-digit core EPS growth toward its $80B 2030 ambition. The dip drivers are known and modeled: Farxiga's US loss of exclusivity and China volume-based procurement, plus a couple of pipeline misses. At $171.49 (verified Oct 23 close), ~19% below the $212.71 high and ~16.5x forward earnings, the price fairly compensates for those headwinds.
