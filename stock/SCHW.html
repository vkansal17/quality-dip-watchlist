🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Schwab's Q3 2026 was operationally excellent — record revenue of $7.17B (+17% YoY), adjusted EPS of $1.69 (+29%), record core net new assets of $190.4B, total client assets of $13.33T, NIM expanding to 3.04% and ROTCE of 44% — and the feared cash-sorting dynamic has stabilized, with sweep cash actually rising $7.1B sequentially to $492.8B. The stock sold off anyway (guidance for FY26 revenue/NIM toward the low end, plus ~$5.5B of prudent but margin-dilutive balance-sheet borrowing), leaving it at $90.58, ~21% below the $114.53 high and ~15–16x earnings versus a five-year average P/E near 23x. This is a price dip on a compounding franchise, not fundamental decay.
