🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Dip pool #204

Barrick converts the gold-hedge bid into cash — record free cash flow funded a 40% base-dividend raise and a policy targeting 50% of attributable FCF — but 2026 is a gap year: gold guidance of 2.90–3.25M oz is below 2025's 3.26M oz, and AISC guidance jumped to $1,760–1,950/oz from $1,581 in Q4 2025 as royalties and consumables inflate with the gold price itself. Q2 production beat (796k oz) and H2 is guided to ramp, yet with gold having just broken under $4,000 the margin cushion is thinner than the headline price suggests.
