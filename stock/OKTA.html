🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #96 (C+)

Okta's fundamentals are inflecting the right way: Q2 FY27 revenue +11% to $805M, cRPO +14% and total RPO +17% (bookings outrunning revenue), FY27 guidance raised to $3.216–3.226B revenue and $3.90–3.94 adjusted EPS, with new products ~30% of bookings and AI-agent identity demand building. The problem is price: after a ~54% one-month run to $213.05, only −8.2% off the high, the stock trades at roughly 50x+ forward adjusted earnings for ~10–11% growth — the re-acceleration is real but already paid for.
