🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #32 (B+)

Sprouts is cheap — about $66, ~12.4x earnings, near its 52-week low versus a $111.83 high, with 34.8% ROE, almost no debt and 42 new stores planned for 2026 — but the core business has stalled: Q2 comps fell 1%, FY26 comp guidance is only −0.5% to +0.5%, and gross margin is compressing on loyalty investment and fuel costs. The Oct 28 Q3 print (guided comps −0.5% to +1.5%, EPS $1.20–1.24) is the test; until comps inflect, only 3 legs pass.
