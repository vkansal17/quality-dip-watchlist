Cisco's business has reaccelerated: record FY26 revenue of $63.3B (+12%), a Q4 beat (revenue $17.25B, +17.6%; EPS $1.22 vs $1.17), product orders +35%, $9.3B of FY26 hyperscaler AI orders, and FY27 guidance set above consensus ($72.2-73.4B revenue, $5.05-5.11 EPS, ~$7.5B AI revenue). After an ~18% pullback from its high, the stock trades near ~21x FY27 guided EPS with a dividend and heavy buybacks; 4 legs pass with only the sector leg mixed.
