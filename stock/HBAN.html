BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Huntington just printed its first full quarter with both Texas-expansion deals (Veritex and Cadence) consolidated: Q3 2026 adjusted EPS of $0.38 met consensus, NII rose ~37% YoY to ~$2.1B with NIM expanding to 3.25%, and management reaffirmed 2027 EPS guidance of $1.75–1.83 with a 17–18% ROTCE target. At $15.73 that is roughly 9x the 2027 guide for a bank whose deal synergies are tracking ahead of plan — the dip is sector sentiment, not fundamentals.
