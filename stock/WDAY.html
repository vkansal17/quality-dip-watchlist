🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Employment & Labor · Pro pool #164 (C)

Workday is the labor-management platform compounding through the slowdown: fiscal Q2 2027 subscription revenue rose 14% to $2.471B, full-year guidance was raised to $9.94–9.95B with the non-GAAP margin target lifted to 31%, and AI products reached nearly $600M of ARR (+200% YoY) and over 25% of new ACV across 5,500+ agent customers. After a steep de-rating, $209.99 is ~17.4x forward earnings and ~17.8x FCF for a 31%-margin subscription compounder — the AI-disruption discount is in the price while AI is, on the evidence, adding ACV. The soft spots are deceleration (FY28 growth guided ~11%, total backlog +8%) and the structural seat-pricing question AI agents raise.
