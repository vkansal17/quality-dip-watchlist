🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #46

CVS's Aetna recovery is real: Q2 2026 adjusted EPS of $2.58 crushed the $1.87 consensus, the medical benefit ratio improved to 87.4% from 89.9%, and management raised FY26 adjusted EPS guidance to $7.90–8.10 (from $7.30–7.50) with revenue of at least $414B and operating cash flow of at least $11.5B. At $93.47 (~12x the raised EPS guide, −15.5% vs the 52-week high) valuation is undemanding. What holds it at HOLD is the 2027 overhang stack: Caremark 340B pressure and below-normal client retention, plus an Aetna Medicare Advantage Star Ratings drop (69% of members in 4+ star plans for 2027 vs 81% for 2026) that TD Cowen estimates as a ~$720M unmitigated 2028 headwind.
