Phillips 66 is converting the map's only Positive/Severe sector into record earnings: Q2 2026 adjusted EPS was $9.41 (vs $0.49 in Q1), it has beaten consensus four straight quarters by an average of ~63%, and Q3 (Oct 28) consensus of ~$12.02/share is up ~377% y/y on surging crack spreads and 96% refining utilization. The stock has doubled YTD to $283.18 and trades at a premium to refiner peers on peak earnings, which caps valuation at mixed, but rapid deleveraging (debt cut from $27.1B in Q1 to $20.6B in Q2) and fee-based midstream cash flows keep the other four legs passing.
