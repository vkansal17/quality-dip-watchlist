🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #116 (C+)

Garmin is executing superbly — Q2 2026 revenue rose 11.4% to a record $2.02B, pro forma EPS jumped 29% to $2.81, and management raised FY26 guidance to ~$8.05B revenue and ~$10.00 EPS on fitness wearables (+25%) strength. But at $293.74 the stock is only ~6% below its $314.28 high after a ~45% YTD run and trades at ~30x trailing / ~29x guided EPS, so the beat-and-raise is already in the price and H2 margins are guided lower on memory costs.
