Why it's on this list

Down 46% from its 52-week high of $115.75, trading at 0% of its 52-week range. 20-day momentum is -7.4% — easing, inside the screen's not-in-freefall limit. RSI 31 says washed out rather than euphoric, and $3,004M of average daily dollar volume makes it a large, liquid name — the dip is in the price, screened against evidence the business itself is broken. It carries a full hand-researched brief below (dip cause, business pulse, analyst consensus, risks).

What caused the dip

Guidance, not the quarter — the second guidance-led drop of the season (shares had already fallen ~11% on Jul 17 on weak Q3 guidance). Q3 reported Oct 20: EPS beat, then the stock fell ~5-6% after Q4 guidance missed and the full-year outlook was cut.

Business pulse

Profitable and cash-generative but decelerating. Q3 revenue $12.87B (+11.8% YoY — in line, but the slowest growth in nearly three years); EPS $0.87 vs $0.82; net income +43.5%; operating margin 34.6%. Against that: Q4 guide below consensus, the 2026 ad-revenue forecast cut to $2.7B from ~$3B, FCF guide trimmed, ~6% of staff laid off and top content executives replaced days before the report.

What analysts say

A downgrade wave. Pre-earnings: Huber Research cut to Underweight ($70); KeyBanc cut its target to $85. Post-earnings: Needham downgraded to Hold; BofA $105->$90, Morgan Stanley $80->$77, Rosenblatt $75->$71; at least 14 brokerages cut targets. Deutsche Bank and UBS stayed constructive ('no new negatives' beyond an ad-rollout delay).

Media & social buzz

Cautious, not a dip-buying chorus — Motley Fool: 'not desperate to add,' would consider buying only below ~19x forward earnings; Zacks steered dip-believers toward ETFs instead of the single stock. Little social dip enthusiasm vs heavy META chatter.

Risks & what would break the thesis

The growth-engine story is what's impaired — view-hours grew only ~2% in H1 2026 against YouTube/short-form competition; the ad business is admittedly 'behind our aggressive target'; Q4 margin guided to ~26% on restructuring costs; the next real catalyst is the Q4 report in Jan 2027.

🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #11

The business is not broken — Q3 EPS beat, net income +44%, operating margin up to 34.6% — but management narrowed FY26 revenue guidance to the low end, guided Q4 below the Street, and cut the 2026 ad-revenue outlook from ~$3B to $2.7B, triggering the downgrade wave. At $65.85 (−43% from the high, ~19x forward) the valuation now compensates for a lot, yet the growth engine that must re-justify the stock — advertising — just missed its own target.
