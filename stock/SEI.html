🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #51 (B+)

Solaris is a genuine beneficiary of the data-center power crunch — over 2 GW under contract and a Sep 8 guidance raise (Q3 EBITDA to $110–130M, Q4 to $145–180M, Q1 2027 initiated at $200–240M) — but at $58.74 the stock still trades around 41x FY26 consensus EPS (~$1.42) and ~46–69x trailing earnings versus ~25x for energy-services peers, so the −29% pullback from $86.19 does not yet create a margin of safety. Leverage (D/E 2.23) is funding a capex-heavy turbine build-out whose returns depend on hyperscaler contract conversion.
