🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #105 (C+)

The fundamentals just printed their best quarter ever — Q1 FY27 (reported Oct 22, 2026) revenue rose 55% YoY to ~$4.4B, non-GAAP EPS jumped 161% to $4.64, gross margin hit ~58%, management guided Q2 revenue to ~$5.1B and raised the dividend — yet the stock has collapsed ~57% from its $799.87 June high to $343.67 as the memory/storage complex reprices and Toshiba's plan to roughly double HDD capacity by FY2028 raises a supply-overhang question. At ~15x trailing earnings the dip looks more sentiment- than fundamentals-driven, and the queue's -53.9% figure is verified (not a SanDisk-spin artifact; the Feb 2025 separation long predates the run). What caps it at HOLD is concentration and cycle position: cloud is ~89% of revenue, the top 10 customers are ~73%, and peak-cycle margins inside a Negative/Severe sector map leave no room for error if supply loosens.
