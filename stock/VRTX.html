🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro pool #109 (C+)

Vertex's CF franchise is still compounding — the most recent reported quarter (Q2 2026) showed revenue up 12.5% to $3.33B with EPS of $4.73 — and Alyftrek extends CF patent protection to 2039 while Casgevy and Journavx build non-CF revenue. But CF still supplies roughly 90% of sales, and at $523.93 (~30x trailing earnings, only ~6% below the $560.25 high) there is no dip discount for that concentration, so only three legs clear pass.
