The offset to the map's Negative/Severe semi rating is company-specific: NXP's cycle is auto/industrial content (software-defined vehicles, edge AI), not the memory/datacenter complex that drove the correction. At $234.92 — ~31% below the $339.95 high and ~20x trailing earnings with net leverage of just 1.5x — the dip is priced against demonstrated, broadening growth.
