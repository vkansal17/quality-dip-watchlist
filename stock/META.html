What caused the dip

Spending/margin shock, twice. Q2 (late July): -9% after the capex guide was raised and operating income fell on legal/severance charges. Q3 (Oct 21): revenue beat, but shares fell ~6-7% on Oct 22 amid a Nasdaq AI selloff, as investors focused on negative free cash flow, $825B of future capex/lease commitments, and a new non-voting Class C share plan.

Business pulse

Strong top line, deteriorating cash profile. Q3 revenue $64.6B (+26% YoY, 17th straight sales beat); Q4 guide raised to $73-76B. But GAAP EPS absorbed a $9.9B child-safety legal charge, Q3 capex of $34.9B exceeded operating cash flow, and FCF was -$2.1B vs +$10.6B a year ago.

What analysts say

Strong Buy on TipRanks (38 Buy / 6 Hold), average target $828.28 (+29%). Morgan Stanley raised its target to $820 (Overweight, Oct 21-22); Bernstein Buy $800; BofA Buy $810. Cautious outlier: BMO Hold, $580.

Media & social buzz

Heavy buy-the-dip social chatter — Instagram creators list META first among 'stocks to buy on the dip'; Yahoo Finance's Oct 24 'Vibe Check' framed Q3 as green-flag vs red-flag with commenters split on cash flow vs the $135-145B capex plan. Engaged and divided, not euphoric.

Risks & what would break the thesis

FCF stays negative if capex keeps climbing without visible AI monetization; margin compression, Reality Labs losses, and legal exposure still growing.

🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #3

Meta's ad engine is accelerating (+26% revenue, Q4 guidance raised) and analysts still see ~30% upside, but Q3 made it the face of the hyperscaler repricing: first negative FCF since its IPO, 2026 capex guided to $135-145B, $825B of commitments, record CDS spreads and a dilutive Class C plan. With the sector leg failing outright under the map, the rubric caps META at HOLD (1 pass, 1 fail) despite the quality of the core business.

Key risks:

  • FCF stays negative for several more quarters as capex runs at $135-145B in 2026 and higher in 2027
  • Class C issuance dilutes existing holders; record CDS spreads signal rising funding-cost scrutiny
  • Legal exposure beyond the $9.89B charge, including the $25B New Mexico penalty on appeal

What would change this verdict: Credible AI revenue monetization or capex discipline would upgrade it; another capex raise alongside slowing ad growth would downgrade it.
