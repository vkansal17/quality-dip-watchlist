🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Dollar General's turnaround is confirmed by numbers: fiscal Q2 (ended Jul 31) EPS of $2.48 crushed the $2.01 consensus on +5.2% sales and +3.5% comps, and management raised FY26 EPS guidance to $7.80–8.00. At $132.69 the stock is ~16% below its $158.23 high at ~16.8x the $7.90 FY26 guide midpoint, with buybacks resuming and trade-down traffic providing a company-level offset to the weak consumer map.
