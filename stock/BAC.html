🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Q3 2026 was optically messy — a $1.2B non-deductible AML regulatory settlement cut GAAP EPS to $0.91 (adjusted ~$1.08) — but the core engine accelerated: NII rose 8% YoY to $16.6B with yield up to 2.16%, loans grew for a tenth straight quarter to $1.25T against >$2T of deposits, credit improved, and management set 2027 NII growth guidance at 8%+ — new guidance, not a raise: the original 5–7% range was the FY2026 guide, since raised to ~8%. At $50.56 (−22% off the high, ~11.7x trailing earnings) the settlement is priced while the NII compounding is not.

Key risks:

  • Regulatory remediation costs grow beyond the embedded run-rate, or final capital rules force a higher CET1 build that slows buybacks
  • FICC/markets weakness persists while expenses ex-settlement are still growing ~7%, delaying the promised operating leverage

What would change this verdict: A Q4 print without operating leverage, or 2027 NII guidance walked back below the original 5–7% range, would move this to HOLD; delivery of 8%+ NII growth at this multiple firms the BUY.
