🔬 Research-agent verdict

BUY deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Q3 2026 was optically messy — a $1.2B non-deductible AML regulatory settlement cut GAAP EPS to $0.91 (adjusted ~$1.08) — but the core engine accelerated: NII rose 8% YoY to $16.6B with yield up to 2.16%, loans grew for a tenth straight quarter to $1.25T against >$2T of deposits, credit improved, and management raised 2027 NII growth guidance to 8%+, above its original 5–7% target. At $50.56 (−22% off the high, ~11.7x trailing earnings) the settlement is priced while the NII compounding is not.
