🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Employment & Labor · Pro pool #277 (D)

Cintas is the cleanest operator in this batch: fiscal Q1 2027 revenue rose 10.9% to $3.01B with 8.9% organic growth, record 51.5% gross and 23.6% operating margins, and management raised FY27 guidance on both lines (revenue $12.15–12.27B, adjusted EPS $5.45–5.54). What blocks a BUY is price and deal risk: at $224.10, at its 52-week high, the stock trades near ~40x the raised EPS guide, and the proposed UniFirst acquisition still awaits FTC clearance. Quality is unquestionable; the entry price assumes it stays flawless.
