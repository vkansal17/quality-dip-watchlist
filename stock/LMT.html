BUY deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Pro pool #243 (D+)

The geopolitics thesis is paying Lockheed in hard orders: Q3 2026 sales rose 11% to $20.7B, EPS beat at $7.97, Missiles & Fire Control grew 24%, and backlog jumped to a record $292B (book-to-bill 4.0x) on the $52.5B PAC-3 MSE multi-year award — prompting a second straight guidance raise (2026 EPS ~$30.35–30.75, FCF ~$7.8–8.0B). At $604.18 the stock is ~18.9x forward earnings for guided high-single-digit 2027 growth with unmatched backlog visibility; the only non-pass leg is the map's Mixed/Severe defense designation, partly offset by the missile-defense backlog itself.

Key risks:

  • F-35 concentration: Aeronautics is the largest segment and any production-rate or pricing reset hits the whole P&L
  • The $8B+ capacity expansion through 2030 and fixed-price development programs can produce charges if execution slips, as in prior years

What would change this verdict: A FY27 budget outcome that cuts missile-defense procurement, or a guidance miss in January, would break quality/catalyst toward HOLD; continued PAC-3/THAAD conversion at current margins firms the BUY.
