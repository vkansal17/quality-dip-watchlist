🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Dip pool #123 · Pro pool #276 (D)

Lowe's is the housing map made flesh: fiscal Q2 2026 sales rose 8.3% to $25.96B almost entirely on the ADG and Foundation Building Materials acquisitions, while comparable sales stalled at +0.2%, DIY transactions fell, and management narrowed full-year guidance to the bottom of its ranges (~$92B sales, flat comps, $12.25 adj. EPS) explicitly on housing trends and mortgage rates. The Pro business and online (+15.7%) are genuine offsets, but they did not stop the guide cut — and at ~16x guided EPS the stock already pays for a housing recovery that has no dated catalyst.
