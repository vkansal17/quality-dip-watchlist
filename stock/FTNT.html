🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Fortinet is accelerating, not decelerating: Q2 2026 billings +33% to $2.37B, revenue +26% to $2.05B, product revenue +52%, and management raised FY26 guidance across billings ($9.35–9.55B), revenue ($8.02–8.18B) and non-GAAP EPS ($3.41–3.47) on AI-data-center and SASE demand. The only non-pass leg is valuation — at $179.99 the stock trades ~52x the FY26 non-GAAP EPS guide with no dip cushion — but 38% non-GAAP operating margins and FCF that tripled to $966M in Q2 support a premium rather than refute it.
