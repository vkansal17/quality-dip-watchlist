🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #15 (A)

CF is benefiting from a tight global nitrogen market — H1 2026 net earnings of $1.34B and adjusted EBITDA of $2.18B, firmer urea pricing into October, and management expecting supply to stay constrained into 2027 — but Q2 EPS of $4.73 missed the $5.63 consensus and the analyst community is unusually split (Bernstein Outperform/$162 vs BofA Underperform/$115 and KeyBanc Underweight/$111). At ~$120.68 the stock is up ~56% YTD and only ~15% below its 52-week high, so much of the good news is priced. Q3 results on Nov 4 are the near-term test.
