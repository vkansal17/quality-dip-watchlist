🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #14 (A)

Reddit's operating performance is elite - Q2 2026 revenue +61% to $805M at a 43% adjusted EBITDA margin, operating cash flow doubled, and it is buying back stock - but growth is guided down to 47-49% for Q3, US daily users slipped sequentially, and the thesis leans heavily on Google (search referrals and licensing renewals). At ~43x forward earnings with three mixed legs, it does not clear the BUY bar today.
