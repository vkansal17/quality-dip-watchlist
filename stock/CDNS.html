🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #22

Cadence is executing through the semiconductor downturn: Q2 2026 revenue rose 24.2% YoY to $1.58B with an EPS beat, backlog hit a record $8.10B, and FY26 revenue guidance was raised to $6.26–6.34B with the $8.05–8.15 non-GAAP EPS guide intact. The offset is price and geopolitics — at ~$316 the stock still trades near 39x forward non-GAAP earnings (PEG ~3.4), and China (~12% of revenue) carries demonstrated export-control whiplash — so the case waits on the Oct 26 Q3 print.
