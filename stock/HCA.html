🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Dip pool #111 · Pro pool #290 (D)

HCA remains the best-run hospital operator — Q2 2026 revenue +8.7% to $20.23B, admissions +2.5%, ER visits +3.6%, adjusted EBITDA +4.6% — but policy is now taxing the model directly: exchange coverage losses shifting patients to uninsured cost ~$400M of pre-tax income in Q2 alone, same-facility surgeries fell 2.3% (inpatient) and 3.4% (outpatient), and management cut FY26 guidance (EPS to $28.70–$30.50 from $29.10–$31.50; EBITDA to $15.4–16.1B). At $469.03 (~15.7x earnings, −14% from the high) the multiple is fair for the franchise, but a guidance cut plus $49.7B of debt caps the verdict at HOLD into the Oct 27 print.
