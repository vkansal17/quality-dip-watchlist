🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Pro pool #215 (D+)

Enphase is the cleanest example of policy as demand curve: the Section 25D residential credit expired at end-2025 and revenue fell ~20% y/y in both Q1 ($282.9M) and Q2 2026 ($291.9M), the company cut ~6% of its workforce, and Q3 guidance of $290–320M leans on ~$75M of safe-harbor pull-forward shipments. The balance sheet is not the problem — net cash of roughly $325–355M, current ratio ~3.45, debt/equity 0.48 after retiring $633M of 2026 notes — but at $31.60 (−56% from $73.74) the stock still trades at ~31.6x trailing earnings on shrinking, pull-forward-flattered numbers, with no confirmed demand bottom.

Key risks:

  • Post-safe-harbor demand air pocket in late 2026/early 2027 as pull-forward exhausts itself before underlying post-25D demand stabilizes
  • Tariffs and weak European demand compress the ~47% non-GAAP gross margin that currently cushions the decline

What would change this verdict: Two quarters of sequential revenue growth excluding safe-harbor shipments, with margins held, would clear the quality leg toward BUY; a guidance cut below the $290M Q3 floor would argue SELL.
