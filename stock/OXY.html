Occidental has transformed its balance sheet: the $9.7B OxyChem sale to Berkshire Hathaway closed Jan 2, 2026, proceeds cut principal debt to ~$11.8B with a path toward $10B, and Q3 2026 realizations are strong (realized oil $85.91/bbl, +32.6% y/y) with WTI above $100 against a ~$51 breakeven. It sits in the map's only Positive/Severe sector and trades at ~14x ex-one-time-gain earnings despite being only ~4.9% off its 52-week high of $67.45 (verified at $64.17 on Oct 23).
