🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #43 (B+)

Post-Family Dollar Dollar Tree is operationally cleaner — Q2 FY26 sales +7% to $4.89B, comps +3.7%, FY revenue guide reconfirmed at ~$20.6B and FY adj. EPS guidance raised to $7.70–8.05 — but the quality of that EPS is in question: $1.31 of Q2's $2.70 came from tariff refunds, Q3 EPS guidance of $0.80–0.95 missed consensus by ~36% as tariff costs return, and traffic grew only +0.4%. At $125.31 (~15x trailing, ~16x forward) the stock is optically cheap versus off-price peers, yet the consensus target of ~$133 implies only ~6% upside and the map's consumer backdrop is negative, so the rubric holds it below BUY.

Key risks:

  • Tariff costs land harder than the Q3 guide assumes, compressing the 3–4% comp-margin model
  • Comp growth decelerates below ~3% as traffic stalls and discretionary mix fades
  • Labor/logistics inflation absorbs the sourcing gains management is counting on

What would change this verdict: A Q3 print showing comps at guide with tariff costs contained would lift quality toward BUY; another EPS guide-down or comp miss would argue SELL.
