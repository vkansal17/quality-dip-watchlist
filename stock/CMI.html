🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip pool #56 · Pro pool #321 (D)

Cummins is a beneficiary of the power buildout, not a casualty of the capex repricing: Q2 2026 delivered record revenue of $9.5B (+9%) and management raised FY26 guidance to +10–13% revenue growth and 18.0–18.5% EBITDA margin, with Power Systems revenue +19% at a 24.5% EBITDA margin, power-generation growth guided at 15–25%, a multiyear hyperscaler agreement covering several GW, and 95-liter genset demand booked into H2 2028. North American truck markets are improving rather than deteriorating. At $517.40 — ~29% below its high despite the guidance raise — the stock trades at a high-teens multiple on FY26 earnings power, making the dip a multiple story, not a fundamentals story.
