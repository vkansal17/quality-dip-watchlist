Expand Energy is the cleanest pass in this batch: North America's largest gas producer has beaten consensus four straight quarters, reaffirmed 2026 production guidance, cut debt to ~0.5x leverage, and returned ~$850M via buybacks year-to-date with a fresh $1B authorization. It trades at ~8x trailing earnings in the map's only strongly positive severe sector (energy), with an Oct 27 earnings catalyst days away; the honest offset is commodity exposure — realized gas prices already caused a Q2 revenue miss.
