🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Geopolitics & Energy Security · Dip pool #107 · Pro pool #149 (C)

SLB beat for a fourth straight quarter — Q3 2026 revenue of $9.45B (+5.8% YoY), adjusted EPS of $0.64 vs ~$0.61–0.62 expected, EBITDA margin up 71bp sequentially, and Q4 guided to ~$10B — but the growth is rotating away from the core: Production Systems (+15% YoY) and Data Center Solutions (+76%) carried the print while core oilfield divisions declined 2–3% and adjusted EPS fell $0.05 YoY. At $53.06 the stock trades around ~25x trailing earnings versus 14–18x for services peers, a premium that prices in the data-center pivot and a 2027 Middle East recovery before either is proven at scale.
