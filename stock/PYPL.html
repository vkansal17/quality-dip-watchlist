🔬 Research verdict

HOLD deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #15

PayPal fails the quality leg: branded checkout - the core profit engine - grew just 2% in Q2, management cut its Q3 growth guide to 1-2% in September, the take rate is eroding, and BofA and Cleveland Research both downgraded to Underperform in October. At ~10.6x the ~$5.38 FY26 EPS guide the decline is heavily priced, which keeps this at HOLD rather than SELL into the October 27 print - but there is no evidence yet for a BUY.
