🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Dip Top 50 #49

Ingersoll Rand's dip is a multiple story, not a fundamentals story: Q2 2026 revenue rose ~9% to $2.05B, adjusted EPS grew 7% to $0.86, orders were up 5%, and management raised FY26 revenue growth guidance to 4.5–6.5% while pointing EPS to the high end of $3.45–$3.57. The stock has de-rated ~26–27% from its high to $73.48 — roughly 21x adjusted forward earnings — while leverage sits at ~1.7x and July orders accelerated to double-digit organic growth.
