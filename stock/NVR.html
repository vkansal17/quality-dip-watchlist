🔬 Research verdict

SELL deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Economics & Growth · Dip pool #67 · Pro pool #532 (F)

The housing cycle is biting the model directly: Q3 2026 net income fell 27%, diluted EPS of $89.45 missed the ~$102.77 consensus, new orders and settlements each fell 7%, gross margin compressed to 18.3% from 21.0% on lot costs and affordability pricing pressure, and cancellations rose to 21%. Backlog units grew 10% but at lower average prices ($453,000 vs $479,500), so there is no evidenced company-specific offset to map #4's Negative/Severe housing backdrop. The fortress balance sheet and buyback keep risk at pass, but quality and sector both fail, which is a SELL under the rubric.
