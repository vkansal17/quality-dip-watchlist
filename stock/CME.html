🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Inflation & Rates · Pro pool #446 (D-)

The contested rate path is CME's revenue line: Q3 2026 produced record revenue of $1.74B (+13%), record ADV of 29.4M contracts (+16.3%) with interest-rate ADV up ~13% to 15.1M, and adjusted EPS of $3.02 (+12.7%), while market-data revenue hit a record $243M (+20%) and cushions volume cyclicality. Valuation is the only soft leg — ~24.8x trailing earnings at just ~7.1% below the high is a premium multiple — but a near-monopoly exchange compounding double digits on the exact uncertainty this theme describes earns the pass on the other four legs. Roughly $1B was returned to shareholders in the quarter alone.
