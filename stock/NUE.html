🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Politics & Policy · Pro pool #552 (F)

The tariff thesis is working in the numbers: Q2 2026 EPS of $4.84 beat the $4.46 consensus on revenue of $10.40B (+23% YoY), Q3 guidance of $5.55–$5.65 is up sequentially and more than double the $2.63 of a year ago, and 50% Section 232 tariffs remain in force with finished steel imports down ~13.5% YoY Jan–Sep 2026 while HRC sits above $1,300/ton. The one soft leg is valuation — at $258.11, only ~7.85% below the high after an ~86% one-year run, the stock prices in peak-cycle steel prices even though the forward multiple (~11–13x) is not stretched. The balance sheet (D/E ~0.3, current ratio 2.51) is the strongest in US steel, so cyclical risk is a multiple question, not a survivability one.

Key risks:

  • HRC prices rolling over from ~$1,300/ton peak levels would compress mill margins quickly, and 2027 consensus already assumes roughly flat revenue
  • Tariff policy reversal or USMCA carve-outs would reopen the import channel the margin shield depends on; Canada's retaliatory 50% tariff adds cross-border friction
  • Q3 guidance midpoint ($5.60) sits below the ~$5.89 consensus, and Q2 results included $130M of one-time raw-material procurement refunds that will not repeat

What would change this verdict: A Q3 miss below the $5.55–$5.65 guide or an HRC break back under ~$1,000/ton would cut quality/valuation and move this to HOLD.
