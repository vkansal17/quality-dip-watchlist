BUY deep-researched 2026-10-25 · Pro Top 50 #28 (A)

Bristol-Myers is executing through its transition: Q2 2026 revenue rose 5.7% to $12.97B with adjusted EPS of $2.04 vs $1.60 consensus, the growth portfolio grew ~15% to ~58% of sales, and FY26 EPS guidance stands at $6.75–7.00. At $61.48 the stock trades at ~9x that guidance with a ~4.1% dividend yield, inside a healthcare sector benefiting from defensive rotation; the dated Eliquis/Opdivo cliffs keep risk at mixed, not pass.
