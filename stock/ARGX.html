BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · supported deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · Dip pool #124

Vyvgart is compounding like a franchise, not a single-launch story: Q3 2026 product sales rose 54% YoY to $1.72B, net profit rose to $506M ($7.80 diluted EPS), and nine-month sales reached $4.53B, funding a broadening pipeline (empasiprubart, FB102 from the Forte acquisition) while keeping $3.7B of cash after paying ~$2.1B for Forte.
