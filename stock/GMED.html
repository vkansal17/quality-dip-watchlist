🔬 Research verdict

BUY ✓ evidence cross-checked · mixed deep-researched 2026-10-25 · 🌐 Innovation · carried from Validated Top 50 research

Globus is executing its post-merger playbook: Q2 2026 non-GAAP EPS of $1.34 (+56%) beat the $1.10 consensus, adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 35.4% from 28.0%, U.S. Spine grew 7% (fifth straight above-market quarter) and International Spine 14%, and management raised FY26 EPS guidance to $4.95–5.05 while reaffirming $3.18–3.22B of revenue. At $81.41, ~20% below the $101.40 high and ~15–16x forward earnings, the multiple is below its historical average despite the margin story. The drags are real but contained: Nevro revenue fell 14.3% and Enabling Technologies 25.8% as it shifts to leasing models.
