How to read the KPIs — the same definitions on every tab
  • vs 52w high / 52w range — how far the price sits below its 52-week high, and where it sits between its 52-week low (0%) and high (100%).
  • RSI (14) — 14-day Wilder relative strength index. Below ~30 reads washed-out, above ~70 extended; this screen wants neither freefall nor euphoria.
  • Momentum (20d) — the stock's own 20-day price change. Rel. strength (20d) — that minus the S&P 500's 20-day change: positive means it is beating the market short-term.
  • VPT (20d) — change in the Volume-Price Trend line over 20 days, normalized: positive means volume has been flowing in on up days (accumulation).
  • P/E — price ÷ SEC-reported diluted EPS (TTM) where computable; blank where earnings are negative or filings don't map cleanly. ROIC — after-tax operating income ÷ (debt + equity − cash) from SEC filings; less meaningful for banks/insurers.
  • Professional factors — percentile ranks (0–100) across the graded pool, blended into a letter grade: Profitability 22%, Revisions 20% (reported EPS-surprise history — the free proxy for analyst revision momentum), Value 20%, Safety 15% (incl. Piotroski F-score), Growth 13%, Momentum 10%. F-score — Piotroski's 9 filing signals. Net debt/EBITDA and FCF yield use industrial-company definitions — read cautiously for financials.
  • Research verdict legs — every researched card is scored on five legs (Quality · Valuation · Sector · Risk · Catalyst), each pass / mixed / fail with cited evidence. BUY requires no leg failing and at least four passing. The dots next to a verdict show the five legs in that order.
  • Analyst consensus / targets — aggregator figures (TipRanks / MarketBeat / StockAnalysis), curated: the crowd's opinion, not a forecast. ⚠ marks a name whose consensus or target contradicts the thesis.
